# Design standard (REQUIREMENTS §20.0)

### 20.0 Professional standard (decided 30 Oct 2026, overrides anything below it)
Every page, public or Studio, must look like a product made by Google, Apple, Stripe or Amazon. It must never look childish or decorative. This applies to every design from now on.
- **Type is small and calm.** Body 14–15 px, secondary text 12–13 px, page titles 24–32 px (rarely larger), section titles 17–20 px. Inter for interface; Gentium Book Plus only for reading text and the occasional title. Never mix many sizes on one screen.
- **Restraint.** No skeuomorphism (leather books, page-turn animations, paper textures), no glows, no spinning mandalas, no novelty effects. Ornament is used at most once per page, very faintly, or not at all.
- **Organisation first.** Every collection has clear navigation: sections, filters, search, dates, counts, and a way to jump directly to any item. A reader always knows where they are and how to go anywhere else.
- **Grids and alignment.** One content width per page, consistent 4/8 px spacing, aligned edges, generous white space, white background (#FFFFFF), thin #E5E5E5-style borders, small radii (8–12 px), subtle shadows only on hover or overlays.
- **Colour.** Neutral greys for interface; saffron (#853500) only for the primary action, active state and small accents.
- **Dense, scannable lists** (like Gmail, Google Drive or Amazon results) rather than big decorative cards wherever there are many items.
- **Mobile is designed, not squeezed**: drawers for navigation, sticky bottom actions where useful, nothing overflowing.
- Before showing a design: compare it against this list.
